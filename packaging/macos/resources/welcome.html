Glacier Riser

This installs the reverse-reverb riser from Ice Cold Sound. Under Customize you can pick which formats go on this Mac. All of them are selected by default:

  • VST3 for Ableton Live, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper, Bitwig, Studio One and most other DAWs
  • AU for Logic Pro and GarageBand
  • Standalone app, if you want to use it without a DAW

The first time you open Glacier Riser it asks for your license key. You'll find it in your purchase email and on your account page.
