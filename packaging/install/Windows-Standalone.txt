GLACIER RISER: Windows Standalone
Ice Cold Sound

INSTALLING
1. Right-click this zip and choose "Extract All".
2. Move "Glacier Riser.exe" wherever you keep your apps, for example
   C:\Program Files\Ice Cold Sound.
3. Double-click it to run. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected
   your PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway". You don't need a DAW;
   pick your audio input and output in the app's settings.

ACTIVATING
1. Open Glacier Riser and paste your license key when it asks.
2. Your key is on your account page at
   https://icecoldsound.com/en/account and in your purchase email.
   One key works on 2 computers at the same time. To move it to a new
   machine, remove the old one from your account page first.

HELP
Something not working? Email hello@icecoldsound.com and tell us your
OS, your DAW and what you see.
