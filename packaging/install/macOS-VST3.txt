GLACIER RISER: macOS VST3
Ice Cold Sound

EASIEST: USE THE INSTALLER
Download "GlacierRiser-macOS-Installer.pkg" from your account page at
https://icecoldsound.com/en/account and double-click it. It puts VST3,
AU and the Standalone app in the right places for every user on this
Mac. Click "Customize" to skip any of them.

The installer isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may say it "can't
be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it. Click Done, then open
System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
"Open Anyway" next to the Glacier Riser message. (On macOS 14 and
older you can Control-click the file and choose Open instead.)

Installed Glacier Riser by hand before? Delete the old copy from
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins first, so your DAW doesn't list it twice.

INSTALLING BY HAND, FROM THIS ZIP
1. Unzip this file. You get "Glacier Riser.vst3".
2. In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G and go to:
     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   (Use /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 to install it for every user on
   this Mac.)
3. Drag "Glacier Riser.vst3" into that folder.
4. Open your DAW and rescan plugins, then look for
   Glacier Riser in your plugin list.

IF macOS BLOCKS IT
Glacier Riser isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may refuse to load it
or say it "can't be opened". Open Terminal and run this once:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/"Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Glacier Riser.vst3"

Then restart your DAW.

ACTIVATING
1. Open Glacier Riser and paste your license key when it asks.
2. Your key is on your account page at
   https://icecoldsound.com/en/account and in your purchase email.
   One key works on 2 computers at the same time. To move it to a new
   machine, remove the old one from your account page first.

HELP
Something not working? Email hello@icecoldsound.com and tell us your
OS, your DAW and what you see.
